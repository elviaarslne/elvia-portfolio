Case Study 03

Personal Finance Tracking and Budgeting System

  • Problem. Income and expenses spread across 12 accounts (cash, four banks, a credit card, five e-wallets), no central system, no visibility into real balance or budget status.
  • Solution. Built a spreadsheet system from scratch. SUMIFS formulas calculate a live balance per account, net of income, expenses, and transfers. A second engine allocates budget as a percentage of total income per category and compares it to actual spend automatically. Cross sheet data validation means a new entry takes one line, not a manual lookup.
  • Result. A self contained tool a non technical user can run for a full year (1,376 transaction rows built in) without breaking a formula, since input and formula cells are clearly separated and protected.
Google Sheets SUMIFS Data Validation Budget Automation Financial Modeling
