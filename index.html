Case Study 01

Event Logistics & Master Guest Tracking

For an institutional launch event, I built the guest operation end to end. I sourced contacts from physical business cards, researched each person's name and designation to personalize outreach, then wrote and sent over 200 individual invitation emails. Every response fed into a master tracker that automatically grouped guests by VIP status, organization type, and seniority, so leadership could see exactly where things stood without asking for a manual count. The event closed with 180+ confirmed guests and zero logistics failures on the day.

Case Study 02

Automated Insurance Comparison Calculator

Insurance packages vary by age, gender, and condition, which makes manual comparison slow and error prone. I built a Google Sheets calculator using lookup functions that takes two inputs, birthdate and gender, and automatically generates a side by side comparison of every applicable package and its benefit differences, ready to hand straight to a client.
